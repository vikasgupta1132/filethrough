Quick Answers

Common questions answered instantly

Is FileThrough really free?

Yes! FileThrough is completely free with no limits, no watermarks, and no account required.

Does FileThrough upload my files anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers. Firefox support is on our roadmap.

Can I use FileThrough for my team/organization?

Absolutely. FileThrough works great for teams. For enterprise features like centralized settings, contact us.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub or email hello@filethrough.com with steps to reproduce.

Can I contribute to FileThrough?

Yes! We welcome contributions. Check out our GitHub repo for the contributing guide.
