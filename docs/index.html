You shouldn't need a CS degree to upload a photo

Every site has different rules. "File too large." "Must be JPEG." "Maximum 1920×1080." So you hunt for an image editor, open a new tab, upload your file to some random site, tweak settings you don't understand, download it, then come back and try again.

FileThrough reads the upload requirements straight off the page and fixes your file automatically. No editor, no extra tab, no guessing.

How It Works

Three steps that happen automatically

1

Reads the Requirements

Detects upload forms and scans for size limits, dimensions, and format requirements — "Max 2 MB," "JPEG only," "1920×1080 max."

2

Fixes Your File

Compresses images, converts formats (PNG→JPEG, HEIC→JPG), and resizes dimensions — all in your browser, nothing leaves your computer.

3

Uploads It for You

Replaces your oversized file with the optimized version and uploads it. You don't lift a finger beyond clicking "Upload."

Automatic Privacy Protection

Your Photos Won't Reveal Where You Live

Every photo you take with a smartphone contains hidden data about you. FileThrough automatically removes it.

What Gets Removed:

  • GPS Location

    Exact coordinates showing where each photo was taken

  • Camera Info

    Device model, serial number, and equipment details

  • Timestamps

    When the photo was taken and last modified

  • Personal Details

    Author name, copyright info, and editing software

Happens automatically. Every image FileThrough processes is metadata-free. No toggle needed, no extra step.

Your Privacy Matters

Your Files Never Leave Your Computer

All processing happens in your browser.

Image compression, format conversion, and resizing run locally using WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded to our servers or anyone else's.

Automatically strips EXIF and GPS data.

Every processed image has location data, camera info, and timestamps removed. Your photos won't reveal where you live, what device you use, or when you took them.

No tracking, no analytics, no data collection.

We don't log what files you process, what sites you visit, or how you use the extension. Zero trackers.

Works completely offline.

Once installed, FileThrough doesn't need an internet connection. Process files on a plane, train, or anywhere.

Open source and auditable.

Every line of code is public on GitHub. Security researchers and developers can verify our privacy claims.
